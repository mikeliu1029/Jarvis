Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant that runs on a single home PC. It is used only by its owner and household members who choose to sign in. It has no other users, no website accounts, and no advertising.

What Google data it uses

  • Calendar: to read events and to add, move or delete events when asked.
  • Gmail: to list, read and summarise messages, and to draft, send, reply to or archive email when asked. Jarvis asks for confirmation before sending any email.
  • Contacts: to look up email addresses and phone numbers.
  • Drive: to find, open and read the user's documents (read-only).
  • Classroom (a student's own account, read-only): to list classes, assignments, due dates, grades and announcements.

Where the data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's PC.
  • To answer a request, the relevant text (for example an email or a list of events) is sent to Google's Gemini API, which writes Jarvis's spoken reply. Jarvis uses Gemini's free tier, under whose terms Google may use submitted content to improve its services. It is not sent to anyone other than Google.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Jarvis keeps a local conversation log on the PC, which the owner can delete at any time.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the sign-in files on the PC.
