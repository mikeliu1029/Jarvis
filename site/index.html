Jarvis

Jarvis is a private voice assistant that runs on one family's Windows PC. It is not a public product: it is used only by its owner and the members of their household who sign in themselves.

With the owner's permission, it reads their Google Calendar, Gmail, Contacts and Drive, and a child's Google Classroom coursework, so it can answer spoken questions like "what's on my calendar today?" or "what homework is due this week?".
